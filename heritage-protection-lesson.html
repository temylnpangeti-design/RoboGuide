🏛️ What Is Heritage?

Heritage includes places, objects, traditions, knowledge and cultural practices that communities value and pass from one generation to another.

Zimbabwe has many important heritage resources, including archaeological sites, historic structures, traditional knowledge and rock art.

💡 RoboGuide Fact: Heritage connects people today with the history and knowledge of previous generations.

⚠️ What Can Damage Heritage?

Heritage sites can be damaged by human activities as well as natural processes.

✍️

Writing & Drawing

Marks can permanently damage historic surfaces.

✋

Touching Fragile Surfaces

Human contact can affect delicate heritage materials.

🪨

Removing Stones

Taking stones or artefacts removes part of the site's story.

🗑️

Litter

Waste can affect the appearance and environment of a site.

🏗️

Uncontrolled Development

Poorly planned development can threaten heritage resources.

🌧️

Weathering & Erosion

Natural processes can gradually change heritage materials.

🛡️ How Can Visitors Help?

Visitors play an important role in protecting heritage. Small responsible actions can help keep important places available for future generations.

  • Follow site rules and instructions.
  • Stay in designated visitor areas.
  • Do not touch fragile rock art.
  • Never write or scratch on heritage structures.
  • Keep the site clean.
  • Report damage to responsible authorities.

🤖 How Can Technology Help?

Technology can help heritage managers monitor, document and educate people about important sites.

Sensors can detect movement, digital platforms can teach visitors, and interactive systems can provide information without requiring people to touch fragile surfaces.

🤖

RoboGuide Protection Mode

RoboGuide can use sensors to detect activity near protected areas, provide warnings and educational information, and record useful incident information for heritage managers.

🚀 RoboGuide Mission: Use technology not to replace heritage, but to help people understand and protect it.

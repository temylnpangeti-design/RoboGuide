Zimbabwe Rock Art

Explore Zimbabwe's ancient rock art and discover how paintings and engravings provide clues about the lives, beliefs and environments of earlier communities.

🎨 What Is Rock Art?

Rock art is artwork created on natural rock surfaces. It can include paintings, drawings and engravings.

Zimbabwe has many important rock art sites containing images created by people in the distant past.

💡 Rock art gives researchers valuable clues about the lives and environments of earlier communities.

🦌 What Can Rock Art Show?

Rock art can contain images of animals, people, objects and patterns.

  • Animals and wildlife
  • People and human activities
  • Hunting scenes
  • Symbols and patterns
  • Possible spiritual or cultural activities

🏛️ Why Is Rock Art Important?

Rock art is an important part of Zimbabwe's archaeological and cultural heritage.

Because it was created long ago, it can help us understand aspects of past communities that may not be recorded in written documents.

🛡️ Why Must Rock Art Be Protected?

Rock art can be fragile. Human contact, vandalism, weathering and other environmental factors can damage these important records of the past.

Visitors should never scratch, paint, touch or otherwise interfere with rock art.

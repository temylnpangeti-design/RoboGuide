Zimbabwe Birds

Discover the famous Zimbabwe Birds and learn why they are an important part of Zimbabwe's history, identity and cultural heritage.

DISCOVERY 01
🐦

🐦 What Are the Zimbabwe Birds?

The Zimbabwe Birds are soapstone sculptures associated with Great Zimbabwe.

They are some of the most recognisable symbols connected with Zimbabwean heritage.

💡 National Symbol: The Zimbabwe Bird is featured on Zimbabwe's national flag.
DISCOVERY 02

🗿 Where Were They Found?

The bird sculptures were discovered at the archaeological site of Great Zimbabwe.

Their discovery provides evidence of the artistic skills and symbolic traditions of the people associated with Great Zimbabwe.

🔎
Learning Checkpoint Remember the connection: Zimbabwe Birds → Great Zimbabwe.
DISCOVERY 03

🎨 Why Are They Important?

The Zimbabwe Birds have become an important national symbol.

They connect modern Zimbabwe with the country's archaeological and cultural heritage.

🇿🇼 Heritage connects the past with the present. The Zimbabwe Bird is one example of how an ancient object can become part of modern identity.
DISCOVERY 04

🛡️ Protecting Heritage

Important cultural objects and archaeological sites should be protected so that future generations can learn from them.

🏛️ Remember: Learning about heritage is one of the first steps toward protecting it.

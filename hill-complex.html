⛰️ What Is the Hill Complex?

The Hill Complex is located on a granite hill overlooking the Great Zimbabwe settlement. It contains stone structures that were built and modified over time.

Its elevated position made it an important part of the settlement and gave it a strong connection to the surrounding landscape.

💡 The Hill Complex forms one of the major areas of the Great Zimbabwe archaeological site.

🧱 Stone Construction

Like other areas of Great Zimbabwe, the Hill Complex contains impressive dry-stone walls. The builders constructed these walls without using mortar.

🪨

💡 Great Zimbabwe is famous for its carefully constructed stone architecture.

🌍 Why Is It Important?

The Hill Complex helps us understand the organisation, architecture and cultural significance of Great Zimbabwe.

Studying places like the Hill Complex helps communities protect Zimbabwe's heritage for future generations.

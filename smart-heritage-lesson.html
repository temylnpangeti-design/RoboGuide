What Is Smart Heritage?

Smart heritage combines heritage conservation with modern technology.

Instead of relying only on traditional methods, technology can help monitor sites, educate visitors and provide information in interactive ways.

💡 The big idea: Technology does not replace heritage. It helps people understand it, experience it and protect it.
⚙️

Technology Used in Heritage

Different technologies can be used to support heritage conservation.

📡

Sensors

Detect movement or activity around protected areas.

🖥️

Interactive Displays

Present educational information in an engaging way.

📱

QR Codes

Connect visitors to additional digital information.

🤖

Robotics

Demonstrate technology while helping monitor heritage environments.

🌐

Websites

Allow people to continue learning beyond the physical site.

📷

Digital Documentation

Record and preserve information about heritage sites.

How Does RoboGuide Help?

RoboGuide is designed around the idea of using technology to encourage responsible interaction with heritage.

The system can use sensors to detect activity near protected rock surfaces and provide educational information to visitors.

The RoboGuide website also provides an interactive Learning Hub where visitors can learn about Zimbabwean heritage.

The Future of Heritage

Future heritage systems could combine sensors, artificial intelligence, digital maps and robotics to help monitor and educate people at heritage sites.

These technologies can make learning more engaging while helping communities understand the importance of protecting cultural resources.

🧠

Artificial Intelligence

Smarter systems could answer questions and guide visitors.

🗺️

Digital Maps

Visitors could explore heritage sites through interactive maps.

🤖

Smart Robots

Robots could support monitoring, education and conservation.

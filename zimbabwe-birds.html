🪨 Meet the Zimbabwe Bird

The Zimbabwe Birds are famous carved soapstone sculptures associated with Great Zimbabwe.

Their distinctive bird-like forms make them some of the most recognisable artefacts connected with the ancient settlement.

ROBOGUIDE FACT:
Eight famous soapstone birds are associated with Great Zimbabwe. Six were found in connection with the Eastern Enclosure of the Hill Complex.

🇿🇼 Why Is This Heritage Important?

Heritage helps communities understand where they came from and how societies developed.

The Zimbabwe Birds connect the ancient history of Great Zimbabwe with Zimbabwe's modern cultural identity.

Protecting heritage therefore means protecting physical objects as well as the knowledge, stories and cultural meaning connected to them.

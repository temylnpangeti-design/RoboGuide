Great Zimbabwe

Discover one of Zimbabwe's most important heritage sites and learn why its remarkable stone structures continue to tell the story of the past.

🏛️ What is Great Zimbabwe?

Great Zimbabwe is an ancient stone-built settlement in southeastern Zimbabwe. It was an important centre of political, economic and cultural activity in the region.

The site is famous for its impressive dry-stone architecture, including the Great Enclosure and the Hill Complex.

💡 Great Zimbabwe is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

🧱 The Stone Structures

The builders constructed walls using carefully placed granite blocks without using mortar.

The impressive structures demonstrate the engineering, organisation and craftsmanship of the communities that lived there.

🌍 Why Does It Matter?

Great Zimbabwe provides important evidence about the history and achievements of African societies.

Protecting heritage sites helps future generations understand and appreciate the history connected to them.
